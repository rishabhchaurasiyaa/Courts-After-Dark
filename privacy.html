Privacy Policy & Data Handling

Transparency regarding browser storage, client data retention, and educational prototype scope.

100% Client-Side Local Storage

Courts After Dark is designed with a strict privacy-first, client-only architecture. The application does not transmit, synchronize, or store personal information, passwords, or booking records on external servers, analytics engines, or third-party cloud backends.

What Data Is Stored in Your Browser?

The prototype stores minimal operational state in your web browser via HTML5 localStorage and sessionStorage APIs:

  • Simulated Account Info: Your registered email and display name (hashed locally using basic encoding for session simulation).
  • Demo Booking Records: Court ID, requested date, time slot, and status (pending or canceled).
  • Temporary Booking Drafts: Uncommitted booking selections stored in sessionStorage until final review.
  • UI Preferences: Your light/dark theme preference and remembered login email.

Data Retention & User Control

Because all records reside locally on your device, you have complete sovereignty over your data:

  • You can inspect all stored entries at any time using your browser Developer Tools (F12 → Application → Local Storage).
  • Clearing your browser cache or site cookies permanently removes all simulated accounts and reservations.
  • No cross-device tracking, profiling cookies, or persistent device fingerprinting is utilized.

Academic Prototype Scope

This software is developed strictly as an academic prototype for university evaluation. It does not interface with real university athletic databases, payment gateways, or campus security credentials.
