Terms of Service & Demo Disclaimer

Conditions governing the use and evaluation of this student prototype platform.

Demo-Only Booking Disclaimer

IMPORTANT NOTICE: Courts After Dark is an educational concept demonstration developed for university coursework. Submitting a reservation on this website generates a simulated client-side record only.

No physical court, hall, field, or campus recreation facility is reserved through this platform. Do not arrive at university sports complexes expecting a real reservation based on tickets or confirmations generated in this prototype.

Non-Production Environment

By using this website, you understand and acknowledge:

  • There is no live server-side database synchronization, physical access control, or administrative approval system.
  • User authentication is simulated for interaction testing; do not enter sensitive or real-world passwords.
  • No financial transactions, booking deposits, or payment processing systems are integrated or required.
  • Facility availability slots are generated algorithmically for demonstration of scheduling mechanics.

Permitted Use & Academic Integrity

This prototype is licensed and presented solely for educational evaluation, design assessment, and user testing. All logos, facility imagery, and sports iconography are utilized for demonstration purposes within academic guidelines.
